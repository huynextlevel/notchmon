NotchMon icon — three answers

The shipped mark is #D0663F. Claude's brand colour is #D97757. That is why it collides with vibe-notch, whose Xcode target is named ClaudeIsland and for whom that colour is correct. Each variant below drops the vendor hue and changes the drawing. An earlier pass kept the shipped silhouette and recoloured it three ways; that is the same icon in new paint, so it sits in the ruled-out list at the bottom rather than up here.

1 · Cut

the icon is the shape

The notch stops being something drawn inside the tile and becomes a bite taken out of it. The app icon's own outline is notched — flares and all — so the identity is carried by the silhouette rather than by a mark sitting in a box. Inside, one level line and nothing else.

Cut34% of the width, 13% deep, flared outward at both top corners
BaseFilled to 32%. A half-and-half tile read as two colours rather than as one notched object.
SaysThis is the notch — not a picture of one
RiskThe real one. A broken squircle can read as a rendering fault in the Dock, and Apple's grid assumes an unbroken silhouette.

2 · Ring

the notch is the gap

The dial the app already draws, with its gap at twelve o'clock — and that gap is the notch. The arc drains clockwise the way every ring in the panel does, so the icon is the same instrument at a different size rather than a logo about it. No rounded blob anywhere.

Arc320° of track, 62% of it lit, gap centred on top
SaysA measurement, and the notch, in one figure
RiskRings are the most crowded shape in the utility genre. It stops resembling vibe-notch and starts resembling every battery and timer app.

3 · Rows

no notch at all

Two quota bars, each a track with a different amount left — the exact element the panel repeats once per agent. Drops the notch entirely, which is the only move here that leaves the whole notch-app genre behind rather than competing inside it. The name already says where it lives.

RowsTwo, filled to 82 / 34% — three thin bars turned to mush at 16pt and read as a list icon
SaysSeveral things being measured at once
RiskReads as a generic chart or a sort icon if the tracks lose contrast. Check 16pt.

The test that matters

Not the 1024 render. 16 and 32 points, beside the icon it has to stop being mistaken for. If a variant still reads as a sibling of the leftmost tile here, it has not solved anything.

Considered, ruled out

The first three drafts: recolour the existing mark. Ink, a level inside the same silhouette, five bands behind it. All three reused the path out of icon.svg untouched, so all three were the shipped icon in a different colour — they solved the palette collision and left the drawing exactly where it was. Rejected for that.

Keep terracotta, change the shape. Solves the resemblance and keeps the lie: a 53-agent tool still wearing one agent's colour.

A camera aperture. Honest about what the notch is for, and it reads as a screenshot utility. The mark would be answering a question about the hardware that nobody asked of this app.

A pixel creature. Notchling already runs in the strip — but vibe-notch's icon is a pixel creature in terracotta. Putting ours on the app icon takes a resemblance that stops at the palette and makes it the subject.
