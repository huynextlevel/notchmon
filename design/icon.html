NotchMon icon — three answers

The shipped mark is #D0663F. Claude's brand colour is #D97757. That is why it collides with vibe-notch, whose Xcode target is named ClaudeIsland and for whom that colour is correct. Each variant below drops the vendor hue and answers a different question with the space that frees up.

1 · Ink

answers: what is it

The geometry is untouched — the notch hanging from the top edge with its corners flaring outward, which is the one piece of shape nothing else on a Mac desktop has. Only the hue changes, to the accent the default theme already uses. The claim: the silhouette was never the problem.

Hue#A8C6F5 → #4A7FE0 — Ink's own accent, the registered default theme
SaysThe notch. Nothing about quota, nothing about how many agents.
RiskA blue-on-dark utility icon is a crowded genre. Distinct from vibe-notch, less distinct from everything else.

2 · Level

answers: what does it do

The same silhouette read as a vessel: full below the line, empty above it. That is the product in one mark — how much is left — and it is the one thing vibe-notch's icon does not attempt, because notifications have no level. Two flat areas rather than a drawn line, so nothing thins out at 16pt.

SplitFilled to 62% of the shape's height — deliberately not half, which reads as a design grid rather than a reading
SaysA measurement, and that the measurement is the point
RiskThe icon states a number that is never true. A user at 12% sees a mark saying 62% — the objection every gauge icon already answers, Activity Monitor and every battery app included.
MeasuredThe split survives 16pt. Written here as a risk before it was checked; the ×4 row shows the upper band holding at 16, because it is two filled areas and not a rule.

3 · Many

answers: why this one

The notch as an aperture with five bands passing behind it. The bands are the app's own five theme accents — Ink, Obsidian, Anodized, Sable, Vapor — and not five vendors' colours, which would be the same mistake five times over. The claim: breadth is the differentiator, so breadth goes on the icon.

BandsInk · Obsidian · Anodized · Sable · Vapor — the themes, self-referential and vendor-free
SaysMany things in one place
RiskThe busiest of the three. Check the 16pt column before choosing it — five bands across 12 points is around two points each.

The test that matters

Not the 1024 render. 16 and 32 points, beside the icon it has to stop being mistaken for. If a variant still reads as a sibling of the leftmost tile here, it has not solved anything.

Considered, ruled out

Keep terracotta, change the shape. Solves the resemblance and keeps the lie: a 53-agent tool still wearing one agent's colour. The colour is the part that was wrong.

Five real brand colours in the aperture. Says "every agent" most directly and implies endorsement by all of them, none of whom endorsed anything. It also breaks the moment tokscale adds a 54th.

A pixel creature. Notchling already exists and already runs in the strip — but vibe-notch's icon is a pixel creature in terracotta. Putting ours on the app icon would take a resemblance that currently stops at the palette and make it the subject.
