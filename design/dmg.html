NotchMon DMG — three windows

The first thing anyone who is not you sees. Finder draws the two icon labels itself, and — measured off a mounted image, with the system in Dark Appearance — it draws them near-black whatever the appearance is, because a window carrying a background picture gives Finder no way to know what is behind the text. So contrast has to be won against black alone, and a near-black background, the obvious choice for an app this dark, is the one thing that cannot work.

What the chosen one turns into

Not one PNG. Finder picks the scale out of a multi-representation TIFF, and shipping only a 2× PNG is why so many DMG backgrounds look soft on a non-retina display. 

design/dmg/background.png      660 x 400
design/dmg/background@2x.png  1320 x 800
tiffutil -cathidpicheck background.png background@2x.png -out background.tiff

Considered, ruled out

Chasing the appearance. The first plan was two backgrounds, one per appearance. There is nowhere to put the second: a DMG carries one background picture, and the measurement showed there was never a second case to serve.

Near-black with the app name set large in terracotta. Handsome, and both Finder labels vanish into it in every appearance. The name in the artwork is not a substitute: the label under the Applications folder is the one that tells a first-time user what the gesture is.

A photographed MacBook. Every luminance in one image, so the labels land wherever the photo happens to be dark. Also 4 MB in a 17 MB download.
